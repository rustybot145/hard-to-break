Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the Hard to Break Program (“the Program”), by Joshua Heald (“Joshua,” “we”), collects and how it’s used.

1. What we collect

When you buy: Whop handles checkout and collects your name, email and payment details. Joshua receives your name, email and purchase details from Whop. We never see your full card number.

When you contact us: we receive whatever you send, such as your email address and message.

2. This website

This website has no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies and no analytics. Our hosting provider, Vercel, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the site secure and running.

Your Workout Builder answers (like your weight, height and test results) are saved only in your browser, on your device. They are never sent to us. You can delete them anytime with “Clear my saved answers” or by clearing your browser data. The calendar file is created on your device. If you use the Google Calendar button, Google’s privacy policy applies.

3. How we use it

We use your information only to give you access to the Program, deliver it, support you and send you updates about the Program. We never sell your personal information, and we never use it for unrelated offers without your permission.

4. Who we share it with

Only the services that run the Program: Whop (checkout, payments and access) and Vercel (website hosting). We also share information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep purchase records for as long as you have access to the Program and as long as tax and legal rules require.

6. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time. Contact us using the details below.

7. Children

The Program is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
