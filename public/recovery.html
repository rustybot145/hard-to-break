Module 1 · Health and Recovery

Stretch & mobility

Daily stretch

Joshua's dynamic warm-up · every day and before every workout · about 10 minutes

  1. 1
    Knee hugs

    10 per leg

    • Pull your knee to your chest for a second, then let it back down.
  2. 2
    Forward arm rotations

    10

    • Big, full circles forward.
  3. 3
    Zombie kicks

    10 per leg

    • Arms straight out in front. Kick a straight leg up toward your hands.
    • Only as high as you can for now. Go higher as you progress.
  4. 4
    Backward arm rotations

    10

    • Full circles backward.
  5. 5
    Lunge with a twist

    10 per side

    • Big, deep lunge all the way to the bottom.
    • Twist in toward your front knee, then pop back up.
  6. 6
    High knees

    10 per leg · 20 total

    • Drive your knees up.
  7. 7
    Butt kickers

    10 per leg · 20 total

    • Kick your heel to your butt. Hands there if you want.
  8. 8
    Side lateral squat hop

    10 per side

    • Squat down. On the way up, shuffle your feet in, then back out, and land in a squat.
  9. 9
    Curtsy lunges

    10 each way

    • One leg steps back behind the other and you curtsy. Step back out and switch.
  10. 10
    Ollie shuffle with harmonic taps

    5 each way

    • Shuffle side to side, tapping your sternum and humming. Shake your body out.
  11. 11
    Gateway squats

    10

    • Squat down. On the way up, close your feet, hop back out and squat again.
  12. 12
    Inchworm with a reach

    5

    • From a push-up position, put your right foot outside your right hand and reach to the sky for a couple of pulses. Switch sides.
    • Walk your hands as far forward as you can without touching the ground, then walk your feet up to your hands. Reset.

Never skip it. It warms up every muscle and joint, and the more you do it, the more range of motion you'll have.

Rest-day mobility

On your rest days · about 40 minutes

  1. 1
    Foam roll

    About 1 minute per area

    • Calves, hamstrings, quads, glutes, then your back.
  2. 2
    Straight leg stretch

    4 rounds · 30s hold + 20s deeper · rest 30s

    • Legs straight, knees pushed back. 30s where you first feel it, then 20s a little deeper.
    • Shake out between rounds. Each round starts where the last one ended.
  3. 3
    Cossack squats

    3 × 15 · rest 90s

    • Wide base. Sit all the way down to one side, then the other.
    • Your heel stays on the ground at the bottom. Hold a band if you need help.
  4. 4
    Deep lunges

    3 × 15 per leg · rest 90s

    • Take a big step so your back leg stretches long.
    • Come down slow until your knee is just above the ground, then press back up.
  5. 5
    Narrow squats

    3 × 15 · rest 90s

    • Feet all the way together. Squat until you're sitting all the way down.
    • Hold a band if you can't get 15 yet.
  6. 6
    Compression raises

    3 × 10 · rest 90s

    • Sit with your legs straight and your hands by your knees.
    • Lift your legs as high as you can, then lower them.
  7. 7
    Elevated pike hold

    3 × 30s · rest 90s

    • Feet up on a bench, hands on the ground, hips high.
    • Push the ground away and look at your feet. Keep your legs straight.
  8. 8
    Tucked German hang

    3 × 15s · rest 90s

    • Chalk up and use a full grip. Bring your knees to your chest and rotate through.
    • Keep your knees tucked and hold. Once 3 × 15s is easy, extend your legs.
