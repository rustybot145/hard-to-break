Hard 2 Break

Stretch & mobility

After your workout · 3 to 4 times a week

  1. 1
    Foam rollYour legs and your back.
  2. 2
    Straight leg stretchLegs straight, knees pushed back. 30s where you first feel it, then 20s a little deeper. Shake out, rest 30s. 4 rounds.
  3. 3
    Loaded straight leg stretchOn a bench with a 25 to 35 lb dumbbell. Same 30s, then 20s. 4 rounds.
  4. 4
    PulsesOn the bench: 10 quick pulses past your deepest point. Rest 30s. 4 rounds. Always last.

On your rest days

  1. 1
    Foam rollYour legs and your back.
  2. 2
    Cossack squats3 × 15. Wide base, sit deep to one side, heel stays down. Band if you need it.
  3. 3
    Deep lunges3 × 15 per leg. Big step, slow, knee just above the ground.
  4. 4
    Tucked German hang3 × 15s. Knees to your chest, rotate through and hold. Chalk up.
  5. 5
    Straight leg stretch30s where you first feel it, then 20s a little deeper. 4 rounds.
