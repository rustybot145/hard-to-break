Module 1 · Health and Recovery

Stretch & mobility

15-minute daily stretch

Every day · 15 minutes

  1. 1
    Foam roll

    30s per area · 2½ minutes

    • Calves, hamstrings, quads, glutes, then your back.
    • Breaks up tight tissue and opens up your range of motion.
  2. 2
    Straight leg stretch

    4 rounds · 30s + 20s deeper · rest 30s · 5 min

    • Legs straight, knees pushed back. Reach down until you first feel tension. Hold 30s.
    • Push into a slightly uncomfortable stretch. Breathe through it. Hold 20s.
    • Stand up, shake your legs out, rest 30s. Each round, start where you finished the last one.
  3. 3
    Loaded straight leg stretch

    4 rounds · 30s + 20s deeper · rest 30s · 5 min

    • Stand on a bench holding a 25 to 35 lb dumbbell.
    • Let the dumbbell pull you down. Don't push yet. Hold 30s.
    • Reach down with it as far as you can handle. Hold 20s. Shake out, rest 30s.
  4. 4
    Pulses

    4 rounds · 10 pulses · rest 30s · 2 min

    • On the bench, reach down as far as you can.
    • Do 10 quick dips just past that point and back up.
    • Always last, once you're fully warmed up.

Hands flat with straight legs can take a couple of weeks to a couple of months. Volume is what moves it.

Rest-day mobility

On your rest days · about 40 minutes

  1. 1
    Foam roll

    About 1 minute per area

    • Calves, hamstrings, quads, glutes, then your back.
  2. 2
    Straight leg stretch

    4 rounds · 30s hold + 20s deeper · rest 30s

    • Legs straight, knees pushed back. 30s where you first feel it, then 20s a little deeper.
    • Shake out between rounds. Each round starts where the last one ended.
  3. 3
    Cossack squats

    3 × 15 · rest 90s

    • Wide base. Sit all the way down to one side, then the other.
    • Your heel stays on the ground at the bottom. Hold a band if you need help.
  4. 4
    Deep lunges

    3 × 15 per leg · rest 90s

    • Take a big step so your back leg stretches long.
    • Come down slow until your knee is just above the ground, then press back up.
  5. 5
    Narrow squats

    3 × 15 · rest 90s

    • Feet all the way together. Squat until you're sitting all the way down.
    • Hold a band if you can't get 15 yet.
  6. 6
    Compression raises

    3 × 10 · rest 90s

    • Sit with your legs straight and your hands by your knees.
    • Lift your legs as high as you can, then lower them.
  7. 7
    Elevated pike hold

    3 × 30s · rest 90s

    • Feet up on a bench, hands on the ground, hips high.
    • Push the ground away and look at your feet. Keep your legs straight.
  8. 8
    Tucked German hang

    3 × 15s · rest 90s

    • Chalk up and use a full grip. Bring your knees to your chest and rotate through.
    • Keep your knees tucked and hold. Once 3 × 15s is easy, extend your legs.
